# Garda Air Support Unit helicopter

The Garda Air Support Unit (with the Air Corps at Baldonnel) flies Airbus / Eurocopter EC135 T2s (fleet numbers
such as 272) and, since 2020, H145s. The game's helicopter is a stylised EC135.

## What the model follows

- EC135 proportions: ~10.2 m fuselage, 10.2 m four-blade bearingless main rotor, a fenestron (ducted ~10-blade tail
  fan) in a shroud with a swept fin above and a small ventral fin below, a horizontal stabiliser with end plates,
  skids on two curved cross tubes, a wrap-round windscreen, sliding rear doors and clamshell doors at the back.
- Garda kit: a camera turret under the nose and a searchlight on the right side under the cabin.
- Livery (2008 to 2017 photos): white upper body, dark blue belly rising over the rear doors to the middle of the
  tail boom and the lower half of the fenestron; a yellow(-green) band along the top of the blue on the 2008 scheme
  (the later scheme has a thin blue pinstripe instead; we keep the yellow for the "blue and yellow" read);
  GARDA in blue on the cabin door with the crest ahead of it; the tricolour on the fin, a blue fin cap; skids and
  stabiliser in blue; the fleet number on the cowling.
